# Important Notice

As of 21 May 2024 UTC, the Author (G.Mgr) has made a difficult decision to **DISCONTINUE** this app development in favor of Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) through the new and improved [#MamaNyoSquad Publishing](https://github.com/MamaNyoSquad/publishing)!

In a statement:

> Upon realizing the existence of PWAs, **I had to make a difficult decision to retire the said repository**. Another reason for such a retirement is that it puts much strain on developing apps via the Android Studio platform where it may be uncertain to be compatible with other platforms, like iOS.
>
> The importance at this current state is to ensure cross-compatibility with all devices regardless of operating systems and device specifications, which is currently possible with PWA.
>
> Don't worry -- the archival of the repository is still ways off in expectations that some web elements dependent on it may break, so it'll give me more time to polish the final preview release (with migrated URLs). I'll send over the archival state in the foreseeable future.

Read more about the 21 May update [here](https://mamanyosquad.github.io/blog/20240521).

## So, what happens next?

As outlined before, it won't be archived immediately as there are still some changes to be made following the website move -- as well as the completion of Inside Story no.7. The Author will be handing out an archival date when that is done over on X at [@VenusVIDiaries](https://x.com/VenusVIDiaries).

Thank you so much for your continued support, and we hope to stay in touch with you again on our [new website](https://mamanyosquad.github.io/publishing)! 💕
